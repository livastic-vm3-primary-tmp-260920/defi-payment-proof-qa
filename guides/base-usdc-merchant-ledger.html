2. What gets flagged?

  • Duplicate event: a repeated 8453:txHash:logIndex cannot increase the claim subtotal.

  • Wrong asset: require the exact native Circle USDC contract on Base, not just the text “USDC”.
  • Wrong recipient: do not count transfers to other wallets or self-transfers.
  • Delivery mismatch: pending, partial, failed and unknown delivery claims are never treated as successful orders.
